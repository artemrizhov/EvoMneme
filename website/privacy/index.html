EVOMNEME / WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This notice covers the EvoMneme website and its public interactive demo. You can read the site and use the demo without agreeing to analytics. The local EvoMneme tools and your own project databases do not send data to this website's Analytics stream.

Who is responsible?

The website owner and controller for its optional analytics is Website operator. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@example.invalid. Please use email for personal information rather than posting it in a public GitHub issue.

Hosting and external links

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Delivering a page necessarily involves sending connection information, such as your IP address, requested URL and browser/HTTP information, to GitHub. Hosting and security processing is separate from optional analytics and is not switched off by declining analytics. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub's processing, retention and contact information.

Following a link to GitHub, Google or another external website takes you to a service with its own privacy terms. This website has no account-registration or contact form; emailing the owner also involves your chosen email provider.

Optional Google Analytics

With your consent, this site loads Google Analytics 4 (GA4), stream G-EXAMPLE000, to understand visits and improve the website. The legal basis for this optional analytics processing is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a), where applicable). Refusing does not prevent access to the website or demo.

Google receives information such as pages and URLs viewed, referral information, interaction events, browser/device information, language, cookie identifiers and approximate location. Your browser also supplies its IP address when connecting to Google. Google states that GA4 does not log or store individual IP addresses; this does not mean that all analytics information is anonymous. Do not put private information in page URLs.

The website code denies advertising consent and disables Google signals and advertising personalization. The embedded graph does not create a separate analytics visit; standalone demo visits can be measured after consent. No Google Analytics tag is loaded or analytics request sent by this website before you allow analytics or while a saved refusal applies.

Google and its relevant affiliates receive the analytics data. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services, the Google Privacy Policy, and Google's data processing terms. Google and GitHub may process information in countries outside your country, including the United States. Their linked terms describe their international-transfer arrangements and safeguards; this website does not promise that all processing stays in the EU.

Data retention

The owner has set this GA4 property's user/event data retention period to 2 months, with reset on new user activity enabled. Under that setting, new activity renews the retention period for the user identifier. The reset does not extend the retention period of individual event records.

This setting is separate from cookie expiry and does not apply to standard aggregated reports, which may remain available for longer. The owner uses these reports to evaluate website usage. Google explains retention, scheduled deletion and the reset option in its data retention documentation.

Your choices and rights

Use Analytics settings at the bottom of this page to reopen the choices, then select Decline to withdraw consent. Withdrawal disables collection, removes this project's analytics cookies and normally reloads the page to unload the Google tag. If browser storage cannot be changed, collection is still disabled on the current page and the settings control says the choice was not saved. Clearing browser storage can remove an earlier saved permission.

You can also block cookies or clear site data through your browser. Consent can be withdrawn at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Significant changes to the analytics notice can require a fresh choice.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object where processing is based on legitimate interests. Email the owner to exercise these rights. We may need information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant records; analytics identifiers do not by themselves tell us your name. You may also complain to a competent data protection authority; see the EEA supervisory authorities.

For more information about data-protection rights, see the European Commission's guidance.
